# 📊 Electro Hub Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

**Electro Hub Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales, orders, profit, discounts, products, customers, promotions, and overall business performance.

The project uses Power BI's data modeling, DAX measures, interactive visualizations, filters, and drill-down capabilities to transform sales data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX**
* **Data Modeling**
* **Data Visualization**
* **Excel **

---

## 📂 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview

The Overview page provides a high-level view of the business with key performance indicators and sales trends.

**Key metrics include:**

* Total Orders
* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Discount
* Sales trends over time
* Product-level analysis

---

### 2. Profit vs Sales

This page focuses on understanding the relationship between sales and profitability.

**Analysis includes:**

* Sales performance
* Profit performance
* Net Sales
* Units Sold
* Discount analysis
* Average Discount
* Product analysis
* Customer/city analysis
* Promotion analysis
* Year-wise performance

---

### 3. Decomposition View

The Decomposition View provides an interactive way to explore **Total Sales** by different dimensions.

It can be used to drill down into product-level and other business dimensions to identify factors contributing to sales performance.

---

## 📈 Key KPIs

| KPI              | Description                        |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Total Sales      | Overall sales generated            |
| Total Orders     | Total number of orders             |
| Total Profit     | Overall profit generated           |
| Total Discount   | Discount provided                  |
| Net Sales        | Sales after applicable adjustments |
| Units Sold       | Total quantity of products sold    |
| Average Discount | Average discount applied           |

---

## 🔍 Key Business Analysis

This dashboard can be used to answer questions such as:

* Which products generate the highest sales?
* How are sales and profit changing over time?
* Which cities/customers contribute significantly to sales?
* How do discounts affect sales and profitability?
* Which promotions generate better sales performance?
* Which products have high sales but relatively low profit?
* What factors contribute to overall sales?
